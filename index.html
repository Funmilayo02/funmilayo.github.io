      <h2>Research & Teaching Summary</h2>

      <p>I specialize in molecular insect toxicology and vector biology, focusing on the molecular mechanisms of insecticide action and resistance in disease-vector mosquitoes, particularly the interaction of pyrethroids with voltage-gated sodium channels. I integrate molecular, physiological, and computational approaches to identify resistance-associated mutations and advance structure-based insecticide evaluation for sustainable vector control strategies. I also have over 6 years of undergraduate teaching experience, emphasizing inclusive, student-centered learning.</p>
